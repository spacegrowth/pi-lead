usage: pilead list [-h] [--home HOME] [--json] [--closed] [--lead SID] [--all] [--all-leads]
                   [--plan]

list leads and executor sessions

optional arguments:
  -h, --help   show this help message and exit
  --home HOME  state dir (default $PI_LEAD_HOME or ~/.pi-lead)
  --json
  --closed     also list closed and dead sessions (the 15 most recently created)
  --lead SID   only this lead's executors (and those with no lead)
  --all        show every executor regardless of owner (the default without --lead)
  --all-leads  also list ghost leads of other projects
  --plan       after the executors, the plan of --lead (default: the calling lead)
