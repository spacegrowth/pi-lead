usage: pilead queue [-h] [--home HOME] [--cancel ID|all] [--deliver]
                    [--trigger {manual,check,settle}] [--json]
                    sid

show, cancel or deliver the packets queued with send --when-idle

positional arguments:
  sid

optional arguments:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
  --home HOME           state dir (default $PI_LEAD_HOME or ~/.pi-lead)
  --cancel ID|all       remove the queued packet with this id, or all of them (a packet being
                        delivered is kept)
  --deliver             deliver the oldest queued packet if the session has finished its current
                        packet; with the trigger manual a closed or dead session is reopened
  --trigger {manual,check,settle}
                        with --deliver: what asks for the delivery (default manual)
  --json                the queue as JSON
