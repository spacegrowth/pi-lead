usage: pilead resume [-h] [--home HOME] [--effort LEVEL] [--force] sid

reopen a closed or dead executor, or a crashed lead, with its conversation

positional arguments:
  sid             the executor's (or the lead's) session id

optional arguments:
  -h, --help      show this help message and exit
  --home HOME     state dir (default $PI_LEAD_HOME or ~/.pi-lead)
  --effort LEVEL  pi thinking level for the reopened session (an executor stores it)
  --force         resume even when it looks alive, its tab is still open, or pi has no session log
                  for it
