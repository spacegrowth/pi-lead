usage: pilead adopt [-h] [--home HOME] [--from LEAD_SID] [--force] [SID ...]

make the calling lead the owner of executor sessions, so their reports wake it

positional arguments:
  SID              the sessions to adopt

optional arguments:
  -h, --help       show this help message and exit
  --home HOME      state dir (default $PI_LEAD_HOME or ~/.pi-lead)
  --from LEAD_SID  every session whose meta.json names this lead (closed and dead ones included)
  --force          adopt also a session owned by a lead that is live, unreachable or cannot be
                   read
