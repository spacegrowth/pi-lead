usage: pilead route [-h] [--home HOME] [--session SID] {retain} reason

open a registered lead's edit-gate grace window, as /pilead:route retain does

positional arguments:
  {retain}
  reason         why this edit is lead work

optional arguments:
  -h, --help     show this help message and exit
  --home HOME    state dir (default $PI_LEAD_HOME or ~/.pi-lead)
  --session SID  the lead (default $PI_LEAD_SID, else $PI_SESSION_ID)
