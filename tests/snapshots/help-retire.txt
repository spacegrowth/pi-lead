usage: pilead retire [-h] [--home HOME] [--force] [--keep-tab] sid

close an executor and write its successor seed (an index of its packets)

positional arguments:
  sid          the executor to retire

optional arguments:
  -h, --help   show this help message and exit
  --home HOME  state dir (default $PI_LEAD_HOME or ~/.pi-lead)
  --force      retire even when it is busy with no report (that packet is seeded as NO REPORT)
  --keep-tab   leave its process and tab running (the tab is retitled `[closed] <sid>`)
