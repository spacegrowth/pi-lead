usage: pilead close [-h] [--home HOME] [--supersede BY] [--keep-tab] [--self LEAD_SID] [sid]

close an executor's tab and mark it closed (files kept)

positional arguments:
  sid              the executor to close

optional arguments:
  -h, --help       show this help message and exit
  --home HOME      state dir (default $PI_LEAD_HOME or ~/.pi-lead)
  --supersede BY   record what took over its work (a successor's sid, or a word such as successor-
                   seed); shown as `superseded`, and `pilead send` refuses it
  --keep-tab       leave its process and tab running (the tab is retitled `[closed] <sid>`)
  --self LEAD_SID  step down as lead instead (the same as `pilead stop LEAD_SID`)
