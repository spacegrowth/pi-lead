usage: pilead [-h] VERB ...

Lead/executor CLI for pi (iTerm2).

positional arguments:
  VERB
    lead-start       register a lead session and create its inbox (idempotent)
    lineup           say which model classes this pi offers and where the lead sits
    auto             turn a lead's autonomous posture on or off, or show it
    tier             set who chooses an executor's model without --model, or show it, or ask
    plan             a lead's written plan: an ordered list of packets and where each stands
    lint             check a packet file before sending it (advisory; --strict exits 1 on
                     warnings)
    handoff          hand this lead's role to a fresh successor lead in a new tab, from a memo
    close-predecessor
                     close the outgoing lead's tab recorded by pilead handoff (by its handle only;
                     ask the human first)
    takeover         move a lead's registration, executors, wakes and plan to another session id
    spawn            open an executor tab seeded with a packet
    send             send a follow-up packet to an executor (relaunches it if closed or dead)
    queue            show, cancel or deliver the packets queued with send --when-idle
    resume           reopen a closed or dead executor, or a crashed lead, with its conversation
    restart          run an executor's current packet again as a new conversation (a successor)
    adopt            make the calling lead the owner of executor sessions, so their reports wake
                     it
    focus            bring an executor's or a lead's iTerm2 tab to the front
    tidy             put every lead's tabs in order: the lead's tab, then its executors
    whoami           say who a session is: a lead and its executors, or an executor and its lead
    list             list leads and executor sessions
    check            print an executor's status (and its TL;DR when reported)
    report           print an executor's whole report (pilead check prints its TL;DR)
    status           print one read-only status line for a lead or an executor
    stats            per-packet rounds, verdicts, report status and tokens, by model
    verify           machine-check an executor's report against its staged diff
    review           review staged work with a separate headless pi on read-only tools (costs
                     tokens)
    refs             accept: make the refs as they are now the packet's baseline (after your
                     commit)
    diff             render an executor's staged diff to sessions/<sid>/diff-NNNN.html
    board            render <home>/board.html from every lead and session
    notify           post a desktop banner: a report is ready, or --test
    turn-end         say what a lead should hear now that its turn has ended (the extension runs
                     it)
    gate             say what the lead's bash write gate makes of the command on stdin (read-only)
    route            open a registered lead's edit-gate grace window, as /pilead:route retain does
    escalate         say where a report that finds no listener goes (the executor's extension runs
                     it)
    close            close an executor's tab and mark it closed (files kept)
    retire           close an executor and write its successor seed (an index of its packets)
    stop             step down as lead: remove its record, route and usage cache (wakes are off)
    keep             pin an executor so it is left alone (close refuses it, prune keeps it)
    auto-close       park this lead's finished executors: close (retire when heavy) the seen,
                     landed or idle ones
    prune            delete the state of sessions that ended long ago (and ghost lead records)
    doctor           check that this machine and state dir are fit to run pi-lead (read-only)

optional arguments:
  -h, --help         show this help message and exit
