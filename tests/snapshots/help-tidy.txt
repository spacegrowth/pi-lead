usage: pilead tidy [-h] [--home HOME] [--dry-run] [--quiet] [--lead SID]

put every lead's tabs in order: the lead's tab, then its executors

optional arguments:
  -h, --help   show this help message and exit
  --home HOME  state dir (default $PI_LEAD_HOME or ~/.pi-lead)
  --dry-run    show the order and what would move; change nothing
  --quiet      print nothing on success, the reason otherwise
  --lead SID   the lead that asks (for the ledger event; default the caller)
