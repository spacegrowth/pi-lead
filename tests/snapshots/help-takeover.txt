usage: pilead takeover [-h] [--home HOME] [--as NEW_SID] [--project NAME] [--force] old_sid

move a lead's registration, executors, wakes and plan to another session id

positional arguments:
  old_sid         the lead whose role moves

optional arguments:
  -h, --help      show this help message and exit
  --home HOME     state dir (default $PI_LEAD_HOME or ~/.pi-lead)
  --as NEW_SID    the session that becomes the lead (default $PI_LEAD_SID, else $PI_SESSION_ID)
  --project NAME  the project of the new record (default the old lead's)
  --force         take over a lead that may still be running
