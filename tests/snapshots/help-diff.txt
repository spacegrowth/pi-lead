usage: pilead diff [-h] [--home HOME] [--open] [--all] sid

render an executor's staged diff to sessions/<sid>/diff-NNNN.html

positional arguments:
  sid

optional arguments:
  -h, --help   show this help message and exit
  --home HOME  state dir (default $PI_LEAD_HOME or ~/.pi-lead)
  --open
  --all        whole staged diff, not just files the report names
