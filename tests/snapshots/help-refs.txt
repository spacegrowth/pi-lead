usage: pilead refs [-h] [--home HOME] [--reason REASON] {accept} sid

accept: make the refs as they are now the packet's baseline (after your commit)

positional arguments:
  {accept}
  sid

optional arguments:
  -h, --help       show this help message and exit
  --home HOME      state dir (default $PI_LEAD_HOME or ~/.pi-lead)
  --reason REASON  why the refs moved (e.g. 'lead committed packet 0001')
