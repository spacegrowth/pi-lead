usage: pilead whoami [-h] [--home HOME] [--json] [token]

say who a session is: a lead and its executors, or an executor and its lead

positional arguments:
  token        an id, a lead's project or a unique id prefix (default $PI_LEAD_SID, else
               $PI_SESSION_ID)

optional arguments:
  -h, --help   show this help message and exit
  --home HOME  state dir (default $PI_LEAD_HOME or ~/.pi-lead)
  --json
